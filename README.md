## The line

> My fridge beeps if its door stays open five seconds, as if the cold air were sneaking off to a better kitchen.

- Characters: 110 (single line, UTF-8, no trailing newline)
- Target: the fridge's door-open beep — a specific, recognisable detail
- Twist: the payoff lands in the last few words (the cold air sneaking off to a better kitchen)
